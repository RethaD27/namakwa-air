How to apply

Everything happens in one short online form.

  1. Open the form

    Tap the orange button above. The application form opens in a new tab.

  2. Fill in your details

    Tell us who is travelling and which seats you need.

  3. Submit your application

    Send the form. The Namakwa Air team will follow up with you about your seat.

Have these ready

It takes only a few minutes when you have your details to hand.

  • Full name of each traveller, as on their ID
  • A contact number and email address
  • Your preferred travel dates
  • The number of seats you need
